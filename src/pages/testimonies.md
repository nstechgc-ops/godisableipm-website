## "I Am a Living Testimony"

![Lindaja Mary Njonga](/images/bz/a19053ce-img-3458.jpg)

Greetings! My name is Lindaja Mary Njonga, and I am a living testimony of God's love and grace in my life and my family's life. Originally from Cameroon, I now reside in the USA, where I have experienced the boundless power of the blood of Jesus.

Currently, I serve as a BSN at Saint Anthony's Children's Hospital in St. Petersburg, Florida. Every day, I witness God's mercy and power at work, and I am humbled to be a vessel for His divine intervention.

I invite you to join our Prayer Line and become a part of our spiritual family. Together, we lift our voices in prayer, interceding for one another and for those in need. Whether you're seeking healing, guidance, or simply spiritual support, our community welcomes you with open arms.

In addition to my work at the hospital, I am an active member of New Destiny Worship Center in Clearwater, Florida. Several years ago, I felt the Spirit of God urging me to pray for others, leading to the inception of God is Able International Prayer Ministries.

This ministry is dedicated to empowering women who have a heart for prayer and a desire to witness God's miraculous intervention in their lives and the lives of others. Though the journey has been challenging, the rewards have been immeasurable.

Through the power of prayer, we have witnessed incredible miracles, including the healing of individuals with life-threatening medical conditions. Join us as we continue to seek God's will and experience His transformative power in our midst.

## My Journey to Becoming a Nurse — From Sister Analta

As I reflect on my journey to becoming a registered nurse, I am overwhelmed with gratitude for the love and faithfulness of God in my life. Allow me to share my testimony with you.

During a challenging period in my life, I faced the daunting task of rejoining my nursing program after failing exams by a narrow margin. Despite my efforts, administrative hurdles and a lack of available spots hindered my reentry into the program. Feeling discouraged and disheartened, I confided in my dear mother and Sister Dorothy, who introduced me to a prayer warrior named Sister Mary.

Sister Mary's fervent prayers and unwavering support became a beacon of hope during my darkest moments. Together, we embarked on a three-day fast, seeking God's intervention in my situation. Through Sister Mary's prophetic words and steadfast encouragement, I found renewed strength and faith to persevere.

Despite numerous obstacles and moments of doubt, Sister Mary remained by my side, reminding me of God's divine plan for my life. With her guidance and the grace of God, I was miraculously readmitted into the nursing program. Through diligence, perseverance, and the power of prayer, I not only passed my classes and final exams but also successfully obtained my nursing license.

Today, I stand as a testament to God's faithfulness and provision. I am humbled to serve as a registered nurse, fulfilling God's calling in my life to be both a healer of bodies and a servant of His kingdom.

I extend my heartfelt gratitude to Sister Dorothy and Sister Mary for their unwavering support and prayers. Their faithfulness has been a constant source of strength and encouragement on my journey.

To anyone facing challenges or uncertainties, I urge you to hold fast to your faith and trust in God's promises. With Him, all things are possible.
