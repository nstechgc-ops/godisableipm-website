God Is Able International Prayer Ministry has been reaching out to women in the communities in need of spiritual, moral and financial support, and giving assistance to families in need. Our family grows every day and we are blessed to have members in regions all over the world.

We have partners in Cameroon through whom we assist the local women, especially widows and teenage mothers, educating them about female hygiene and healthcare, and giving medical assistance as well. In partnership with members in Germany, God Is Able International Prayer Ministry provides funding and assistance to educate local women on business and craftsmanship, so as to enable these women to be self-sufficient and support their families.
