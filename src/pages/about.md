## Our Mission

At God Is Able International Prayer Ministries, our mission is rooted in serving our community and spreading the Gospel of Jesus Christ. We believe in empowering others to discover their true purpose through fellowship with the Holy Spirit and the study of God's word (Matthew 28:18-20).

**Our Beliefs:** We firmly believe in the transformative power of prayer, healing, and miracles through the mighty hand of God. As a ministry, we stand united in supporting and encouraging one another with the word of God as we navigate the challenges of daily life (Galatians 6:2).

**Prayer Support:** Our prayer lines are open 24/7, and there is always someone available to fellowship and pray with you. Please do not hesitate to reach out to us. Your prayer requests are welcomed and valued as we journey together in faith (Luke 18:1).

## Sister Lindaja Mary

Sister Lindaja Mary is the visionary founder of God Is Able International Prayer Ministry. With unwavering faith and the support of the amazing women and men within the ministry, Sister Lindaja has brought this divine calling to fruition. We extend our heartfelt gratitude to everyone who has supported us on this journey. May the grace and favor of the Lord be upon you and your families, now and always. Amen!
